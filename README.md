Hello! This is Flora, Norah and Musarrat, the creator of 'Gakuen High School Basketball Club'! (a mouthful, we know...)

This game follows Lilico's journey as she navigates her new school, bonds with her teammates and hopefully secure the win (or not) in the basketball championship. 

Here is the itch.io link:
https://m1rrarr.itch.io/gakuen-high-school-basketball-club

This is a visual novel. To play it, you click on the screen and wait for the next text box to appear. 
You can also use save/load through the bottom bar :)
